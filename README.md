MindMate is a student-focused mental wellness and productivity platform designed to support emotional health, peer connection, and guided self-improvement. It combines AI-powered assistance, community interaction, and administrative tools into a single, easy-to-use system.

---

## 🌟 Overview

MindMate helps students:

* Track their mental well-being
* Connect with friends and communities
* Interact with an AI companion (Aura AI)
* Book wellness sessions
* Access helpful resources

At the same time, it provides admins with full control over users, content, sessions, and platform insights.

---

## 🚀 Key Features

### 👤 Student (User) Side

* **Authentication**: Secure Sign Up & Login
* **User Dashboard**:

  * Weekly mental health check-ins
  * Book and manage sessions
* **Aura AI**:

  * AI-powered mental health companion
  * Chat-based interaction
  * AI Avatar with voice/video interaction
* **Friend System**:

  * Chat with friends
  * Voice & video calling
* **Community**:

  * Join group discussions
  * Share thoughts and experiences
* **Resources**:

  * Access mental health content and learning materials
* **Privacy Focused**:

  * Temporary chats supported
  * No unnecessary data storage

---

### 🛠️ Admin Side

* **Admin Dashboard**
* **User Management**
* **Session & Booking Management**
* **Content & Resource Control**
* **Community Moderation**
* **Analytics & Insights** (usage, engagement, activity)

---

## 🧩 Platform Flow

1. Index / Landing Page
2. Sign Up / Login
3. User Dashboard
4. Weekly Check-in & Session Booking
5. Aura AI Interaction
6. AI Avatar Conversation
7. Friend Chat & Calls
8. Resources & Community
9. Admin Panel

---

## 🧠 Aura AI

Aura AI is the heart of MindMate:

* Provides emotional support through conversation
* Uses natural language interaction
* Supports text, voice, and avatar-based communication
* Designed to feel friendly, safe, and non-judgmental

---

## 🔐 Security & Privacy

* Secure authentication
* User-first privacy design
* Minimal data storage
* Safe AI interaction guidelines

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack (High Level)

* **Frontend**: HTML, CSS, JavaScript
* **Backend**: Python (Flask / FastAPI)
* **AI Integration**: NLP-based conversational AI
* **Database**: NoSQL-based (for users, sessions, admin data) (MongoDB)
* **Memory**: SQL-based (Aura AI chat) (SQLite)
* **Real-Time Features**: WebSockets / APIs
